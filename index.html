01ABOUT THE PRACTICE

A multi-disciplinary approach to design.

Architectural Design Lab is a young multi-disciplinary practice offering consultancy services in architecture, interior design and construction.

Each discipline can be engaged on its own, or combined into a single, coordinated service — configured around what your project actually needs, not a fixed way of working.

The practice has the capacity, experience and expertise to take on large projects, while remaining open to small and medium-scale assignments.

05PHILOSOPHY

We make life art of design.

Architecture is more than meeting business objectives. It shapes how people experience the environments they live, work and gather in.

The practice sees architecture as physical and intellectual — but also emotional: creating connections between people and their changing environments. Environments that welcome travellers, invite shoppers and support learners.

The goal, always: enhance life by design.
